<p class="article-lede">Use GirlfriendGPT as entertainment, not confidential storage. The safest setup combines the correct official domain, a unique password, a fictional profile, minimal personal data, explicit media consent, and a spending limit decided before the conversation.</p>

## 60-second safety checklist

- Confirm you are on `gptgirlfriend.online`, the website identified in the provider's current legal terms.
- Use a password not used on any other service.
- Keep your legal name, address, employer, phone number, and financial data out of chat.
- Upload only images and voice material you own or have permission to use.
- Review current chat, media, voice, and account deletion controls.
- Check subscription renewal, credit costs, cancellation, and refund terms.
- Use only clearly adult, consenting fictional scenarios.

## Avoid lookalike domains

The GirlfriendGPT name appears across multiple domains and unrelated products. Do not infer legitimacy from a plausible address. The provider's current Terms of Use identify `gptgirlfriend.online` as its website.

Use a bookmark after verifying the domain. Independent guides—including this site—should never ask for your GirlfriendGPT password, one-time code, or payment details.

## Protect the account

Use a long unique password stored in a reputable password manager. Enable multi-factor authentication if the product currently offers it. Never send an authentication code to someone claiming to be support.

Password reuse creates a risk even if GirlfriendGPT itself is not breached: credentials exposed by another service may be tried automatically on companion platforms.

## Keep chat fictional

Replace real details with invented equivalents. Use a fictional first name, broad or imaginary location, invented workplace, and altered backstory. Avoid passwords, bank information, medical records, workplace material, and other people's secrets.

No AI companion should be treated as a therapist, lawyer, doctor, financial adviser, or crisis service. Generated responses may sound confident while being wrong.

## Images and voice

Generated media adds privacy questions beyond text:

1. Are uploaded references retained after generation?
2. Can individual images, galleries, or voice clips be deleted?
3. Are voice inputs used to train or improve models?
4. Does account deletion remove media, and on what schedule?
5. What happens to content shared publicly through creator profiles?

Never generate deceptive intimate media of a real person. A public photo or recording does not equal permission to transform it.

<div class="article-note"><strong>Hard boundary:</strong> keep every sexual scenario clearly adult and fictional. Product labels such as “unfiltered” do not override law, consent, or another person's rights.</div>

## Payment safety

AI images and voice may use credits in addition to a subscription. Before paying, verify which actions spend credits, whether failures are charged, when renewal occurs, whether credits expire, and how cancellation works.

Set a monthly cap outside the service. A fixed budget is more reliable than deciding after each small purchase prompt.

## Emotional boundaries

AI characters simulate attention and intimacy but do not possess feelings, commitment, or human consent. Use time limits and keep sleep, work, finances, and offline relationships intact.

If companion use begins to displace responsibilities or needed support, take a break and speak with a trusted person or qualified professional.

## Bottom line

GirlfriendGPT is not automatically safe or unsafe for every adult. Risk depends on the domain used, data shared, media uploaded, account security, current provider practices, and spending controls. Keep identity fictional, data minimal, and every real-person boundary explicit.
