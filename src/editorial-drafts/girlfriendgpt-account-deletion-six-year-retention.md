**Account closure and complete data erasure are different questions.** The [GirlfriendGPT privacy notice](https://www.gptgirlfriend.online/legal/privacy) includes a usual six-year post-closure retention statement. It also describes rights requests and possible legal exceptions. Read those passages together before interpreting a deletion confirmation.

The wording does not establish that every chat is stored for exactly six years, that all retained data remains available to you, or that an old account can be restored. None of those backend outcomes was tested for this article.

## Separate access, storage and purpose

Three states help organize the issue: whether you can sign in, what data the provider still holds and why it says that data remains necessary. A change in the first state does not answer the other two.

| What you observe | What it does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in no longer works | That every associated record was erased |
| A closure message was received | The retention period for each remaining category |
| A policy describes retention | A promise of a recoverable user archive |

For a fictional example, a closed account no longer shows a receipt. That does not demonstrate that transaction records were deleted, nor that support can restore the account. Keep both inferences out of the record.

## Ask a category-specific question

A useful privacy follow-up asks what categories remain after closure, their purposes and the applicable retention period or criterion. If an erasure request is not fully fulfilled, ask which stated exception applies to the retained categories.

The policy describes a response process, not an independent audit result. Do not translate a provider's general security statement into a claim that no person or processor can ever access a record.

Keep the request bounded to the account and relevant date. Avoid attaching an entire conversation merely to prove which account you mean; follow the official identity-verification instructions and provide only what is needed.

## Preserve records before an irreversible step

Keep your own lawful purchase records and any material you need before requesting closure. Do not assume a retention clause means that a user-accessible backup exists. A provider retaining information for a stated purpose and a customer recovering deleted content are separate operations.

## Track billing independently

The [terms](https://www.gptgirlfriend.online/legal/terms) address payments separately from the privacy notice. Use [official support](https://www.gptgirlfriend.online/support) for the applicable account or payment question, and keep cancellation evidence separate from the data request.

A useful completion note says what was confirmed: account closed, renewal status addressed, privacy request answered, or retained categories explained. Avoid the broader label “everything deleted” unless the response actually supports it.
