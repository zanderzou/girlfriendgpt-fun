Girlfriend GPT vs Kindroid for lore steering is not just a question of how much text you can enter. A useful character definition separates what is true in the fictional world, how the character speaks and what is happening right now. Mixing those categories makes revisions harder to diagnose.

This article reviews official creation information on October 8, 2026. It proposes an original non-explicit writing exercise with adult fictional characters, not a tested ranking of instruction adherence.

## Give each kind of instruction a job

Imagine an adult fictional astronomer, Ellis, writing letters from an island observatory. The observatory has no electric lighting. Ellis speaks in plain, brief sentences. Tonight, a delivery of paper has arrived but remains unopened.

The absence of electric lighting is world canon. Brief sentences are dialogue style. The unopened delivery is temporary scene state. If you later open the delivery, that event should not change the observatory's infrastructure or Ellis's voice.

| Category | Example | Revision rule |
|---|---|---|
| World fact | The observatory uses oil lamps | Change only when the fictional canon changes |
| Character role | Ellis studies tides and stars | Keep stable during this scene |
| Reply style | Plain, brief sentences | Adjust separately from world facts |
| Current event | The delivery is unopened | Update as the scene advances |
| Player agency | The visitor chooses what to open | Preserve the unresolved decision |

## Inspect the editable definition

Girlfriend GPT exposes an advanced creation route. Inspect its current fields and what you can edit for your own character. A public character's visible card may not reveal every instruction used in a conversation, and selecting it does not give you the same control as authoring your own definition.

Kindroid documents personality controls. Map the same original brief to the supported setup without assuming field names or account access are identical across products. Keep a local copy so you can review the exact text you supplied.

## Change one layer and watch the others

First, ask Ellis to describe the room while the delivery remains unopened. Check for invented electric lights or a decision that the visitor already opened the package. Those are separate failures from an overly long reply.

Next, revise only the style request to make the reply more concise. Ask another room question and check whether the world still uses oil lamps. If the response becomes shorter but adds electric lighting, the style improvement did not preserve the full brief.

## Avoid repairing every issue with a bigger profile

When a reply drifts, identify whether the problem concerns a world fact, a style choice or a temporary event. A short correction to the relevant layer is easier to assess than replacing the entire persona with a much longer description.

Do not import a real person's private biography to make the exercise feel detailed. Original fictional constraints supply enough evidence for this comparison.
