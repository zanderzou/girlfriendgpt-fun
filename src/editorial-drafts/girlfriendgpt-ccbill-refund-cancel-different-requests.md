**A refund request concerns a past payment; cancellation concerns future renewal.** For a GirlfriendGPT purchase, identify the payment provider before deciding where to send either question. Do not assume that every receipt uses the same processor.

The [GirlfriendGPT terms](https://www.gptgirlfriend.online/legal/terms) route CCBill refund requests to that provider and other-method refund requests to GirlfriendGPT support. Their general policy is restrictive, with limited exceptions and a qualification for applicable local rules. That is not a promise that a particular request will be approved.

## Match the original transaction

Find the merchant or processor, transaction reference, charge date and account context in your existing receipt. A screenshot of a pricing page does not identify which system took your payment.

Keep the record private. This independent publication cannot search a provider's billing database or change your subscription. Do not send a password, verification code or complete card number to a public comment thread seeking help.

## Keep two requested outcomes visible

| Request | Confirmation to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Stop renewal | The subscription concerned and its future billing status |
| Return a charge | The transaction concerned, decision and approved amount if applicable |

For an illustrative case, a support message says a refund inquiry was received. That establishes receipt of the inquiry, not a decision on the refund and not confirmation that renewal stopped. Keep both questions open until the relevant response addresses them.

## Use the provider-specific route

[CCBill's consumer support page](https://support.ccbill.com/) offers subscription lookup and cancellation assistance. Use it when your actual payment record identifies CCBill. Navigate to the official domain independently rather than following a stranger's recovery link.

For a different payment method, use the [official GirlfriendGPT support route](https://www.gptgirlfriend.online/support) and explain which transaction is involved. If the purchase was through another platform, identify that platform rather than trying to force its reference into a CCBill form.

The terms' refund wording should not be turned into a universal cooling-off promise. A review period or an exception is different from an unconditional entitlement. State the factual reason for the request and retain the response.

## Read what the confirmation actually covers

Save the reference for each interaction and the exact subscription or payment it concerns. If the response is ambiguous, ask whether future billing is stopped and whether the past transaction has a separate decision.

Deleting a login, removing an app or no longer using the service does not by itself document those billing outcomes. A clear final record contains the processor, affected subscription, renewal status and any refund decision, without treating one message as evidence of actions it never mentions.
